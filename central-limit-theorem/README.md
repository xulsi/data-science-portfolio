# Central Limit Theorem — Visual Demonstration

## Overview
Empirical demonstration of one of the most important theorems
in statistics using Python simulation.

## Key Concept
The CLT states that the distribution of sample means approaches
normality as sample size increases, regardless of the original
population distribution.

## What the notebook shows
- Original population: strongly right-skewed (exponential)
- Sample means (n=5): more symmetric but still skewed
- Sample means (n=30): approximately normal

## Why this matters
The CLT is the theoretical foundation behind:
- Hypothesis testing (Z-test, T-test)
- Confidence intervals
- Most machine learning algorithms
